# mysterflags

Mysterflags is a simple countries' flag quiz designed for people who want to memorize countries' flags in a fun, competitive, and simple way.
Mysterflags was developed by Jo7ico, who loves geography and geopolitics. He made this entirely free to use (and forever) with no signups or logins required at all.

_Why Mysterflags specifically?_
1. It is completely free and doesn't require logins or signups
2. People can test their knowledge in flags of countries in different regions, such as countries in the Arab World, Latin America, Former colonies of the British Empire, and countries in different continents.
3. People may guess up to 197 countries in a single quiz
4. Mysterflags is super secure, as people won't be logging in and that the creator doesn't want any of your information
5. It shows what flags users got wrong/correct when they finish a quiz (_no, what questions you got wrong or correct will NOT be stored whatsoever_)

Master flag knowledge with Mysterflags.

**Special Features of Mysterflags**
1. Offers light more and dark mode for users who want the quiz to be bright or darker
2. People can test their knowledge of flags in different regions and continents
3. It shows what flags users got wrong/correct at the end of the quiz
4. People may choose how many questions they want to have in their quizzes
